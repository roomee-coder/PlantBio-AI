Meet your PlantBio AI assistant

Ask scientific questions in natural language and connect answers to your curated knowledge base and literature.

Ask a research question

"My banana explants are browning after 5 days. What could be causing it?"
"Compare BAP and TDZ for shoot multiplication."
"Find research gaps in turmeric micropropagation."
AI analysis

Browning may be associated with phenolic oxidation, explant injury, culture conditions or other factors. Consider reviewing the explant source, sterilization stress and medium/PGR conditions.

Suggested investigation
  • Compare younger vs. older explants.
  • Record browning over time.
  • Compare suitable antioxidant treatments reported in literature.
  • Check medium pH and culture conditions.
